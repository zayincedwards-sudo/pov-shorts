pov-shorts

A personal tool for publishing short-form video to its operator's own YouTube channels.

pov-shorts is a private automation tool. It assembles short vertical videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned by the person running it. It is not a product, not a service, and has no users other than its operator.

What it does

  • Searches publicly available YouTube videos for candidate clips.
  • Selects a short moment from a candidate and writes a caption for it.
  • Renders a vertical (1080×1920) video with the caption shown above the clip.
  • Uploads finished videos to the operator's own YouTube channels.
  • Reads public view counts for the operator's own uploads to inform later captions.

Why it asks for YouTube access

Uploading a video to a YouTube channel requires that channel's own authorisation. The YouTube Data API has no parameter for choosing a destination channel — an upload always goes to the channel whose credentials were used. The tool therefore asks the operator to sign in once per channel, and requests only:

  • youtube.upload — to publish a video to the signed-in channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to confirm which channel a sign-in belongs to, so a video cannot be uploaded to the wrong one by mistake.

It does not request analytics access, and cannot read watch time, retention, demographics, or traffic sources.
